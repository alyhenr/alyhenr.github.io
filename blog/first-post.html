Intro

Welcome to your blog. This is a template post — replace it with your own writing. The structure is intentionally minimal: a title, some metadata, and then your content.

Section heading

Use <h2> elements inside .prose as section headings. Body copy sits on the light document surface of the window, so it stays comfortable to read at length.

Inline code goes in <code> tags. Longer samples go in a <pre> block like the one below.
