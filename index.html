browser extensions
Alvarás — controle de vencimento

A browser extension for civil engineers in Brazil. It reads a construction permit's grant date from the municipal portal in one click and keeps every permit in one list, sorted by whichever expires first.

No account, no server, nothing transmitted — everything stays in your own browser. Written in Portuguese, for Brazilian municipal portals.
